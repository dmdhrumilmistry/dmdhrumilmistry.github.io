About me

I break things for a living so they don't break in production. At BrowserStack I work on zero trust access, supply chain security and the internal tools our security team runs every day. I also lead OWASP OFFAT, an open source tool that turns an OpenAPI spec into security tests and runs them against your API.

Most of what I know came from building offensive tools in the open, hunting account takeover bugs in the APIs of government bodies, companies and startups, and then automating each finding so the same class of bug can't come back.

I studied Electronics and Telecommunication, which is why I still reach for C, assembly and an Arduino when a problem gets interesting.
